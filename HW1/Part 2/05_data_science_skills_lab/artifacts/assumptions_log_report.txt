--- BUSINESS LOGIC ---
  #4 [HIGH conf / HIGH impact | risk=5]
    Cross-sectional cohort reconstruction (join_date = snapshot_date - tenure) systematically misplaces churned customers into more-recent cohort buckets than their true signup quarter, biasing older reconstructed cohorts' survival curves upward.
    Rationale: Directly measured: oldest reconstructed cohort (2014Q1, n=434) sits +24.38pp above the pooled life-table baseline at matched tenure months — confirms the predicted direction and gives a quantified magnitude (cohort-analysis.md).
    Validation: ✗ not validated
  #5 [HIGH conf / CRITICAL impact | risk=6]
    The Contract-vs-churn comparison (month-to-month vs. two-year) is observational and cannot be interpreted as the causal effect of switching a customer's contract length.
    Rationale: No randomization occurred; customers self-selected their contract. Confirmed by design: SRM check on the two groups is non-diagnostic here (no randomization to break), and the gap shrinks from +39.88pp unadjusted to +36.09pp after tenure-stratification but does not disappear, consistent with residual confounding (ab-test-analysis.md).
    Validation: ✗ not validated
  #6 [HIGH conf / MEDIUM impact | risk=3]
    k=3 is used for the customer segmentation instead of the silhouette-optimal k=2.
    Rationale: k=2 scores a higher silhouette (0.3369 vs. k=3's 0.3075), but produces a split too coarse to assign differentiated retention strategies to (2 groups vs. 3-7 the skill's own process recommends as actionable). k=3's 0.3075 still clears the skill's 0.3 validity bar (segmentation-analysis.md).
    Validation: ✗ not validated
  #7 [MEDIUM conf / CRITICAL impact | risk=7]
    Two conflicting customer lifetime value (LTV) figures exist — hazard-based ($7,899.96, implying ~122 months of expected lifetime) and tenure-based empirical ($2,283.30) — and the tenure-based figure is treated as the defensible one pending Phase 5's explicit ruling.
    Rationale: The hazard-based estimate (ARPU / monthly hazard rate) is biased upward by single-snapshot survivorship: it implicitly assumes today's still-active customers' future hazard mirrors the population average forever, ignoring right-censoring at the observed 72-month tenure ceiling. business_metrics.json documents both formulas explicitly (ltv section).
    Validation: ✗ not validated
