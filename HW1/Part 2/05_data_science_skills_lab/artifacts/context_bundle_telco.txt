# Context Bundle

## TASK
Build a churn-risk model and monthly ranked risk list for the Telco Customer Churn
dataset (data/Telco-Customer-Churn.csv, 7,043 customers) so the retention team can
prioritize outreach. Follow the full CRISP-DM cycle (phases 1-6, plan in
crisp_dm/01_business_understanding/analysis-planning.md). For any downstream task,
produce output as: a ranked customer list (customerID, churn_probability,
recommended_action) plus a short narrative of the top risk drivers. Use only the
columns present in data/Telco-Customer-Churn.csv / data/processed/{train,test}.csv.

## BUSINESS CONTEXT
### business.md
A telecom subscription business wants to reduce voluntary churn. The retention team
can act on a ranked risk list monthly — this is an operational, recurring decision
(not a one-off strategic call), so favor a fast, actionable, directionally-correct
output over an exhaustive one (see decision_maker_framework.md calibration).

Key metric definitions (computed for real in artifacts/business_metrics.json):
- MRR: sum of MonthlyCharges, treated as each customer's recurring subscription
  price / MRR contribution. All-customer MRR = $456,116.60/mo; active-only = $316,985.75/mo.
- ARPU: $64.76/mo all customers ($61.27 active-only); by contract: Month-to-month
  $66.40, One year $65.05, Two year $60.77.
- Logo churn rate (base, whole-tenure): 26.537% (matches data/processed/dataset_meta.json).
- Revenue churn rate (base): 30.503% — churn skews toward higher-MonthlyCharges accounts.
- Monthly churn hazard rate (empirical, from customer-months of tenure): 0.82%/month.
- LTV: churn-rate-based $7,899.96 (ARPU / monthly hazard, no margin data available);
  tenure-based empirical (mean TotalCharges) $2,283.30.
- Revenue at risk: $139,130.85 already realized (churned customers' MRR, 30.5% of
  total MRR); $136,447.05 forward-looking exposure (active Month-to-month MRR, the
  highest-churn segment, 43.05% of active MRR).
- tenure = months since acquisition. Churn = voluntary churn flag (Yes/No raw,
  0/1 processed).

Business context: mid-size residential telecom provider. Product lines: phone
service (+ multiple lines), internet service (DSL / Fiber optic / none) with six
internet-dependent add-ons, billing via 4 payment methods, and 3 contract terms
(Month-to-month / One year / Two year). Month-to-month customers churn at 42.71%
vs. 11.27% (One year) and 2.83% (Two year) — contract term is the strongest
observed churn-risk dimension so far (Phase 1, pre-model).

## PRIOR FINDINGS
### prior_findings.md
From Phase 1 (Business Understanding, this lab):
- Contract term is the strongest observed churn-risk dimension: Month-to-month
  42.71% logo churn vs. One year 11.27% vs. Two year 2.83%
  (artifacts/business_metrics.json:churn.logo_churn_rate_by_contract).
- Revenue churn (30.50%) exceeds logo churn (26.54%) — churn is concentrated
  somewhat more in higher-MonthlyCharges accounts, raising the dollar stakes of
  the retention program beyond what a simple customer-count churn rate implies.
- 43.05% of currently-active MRR sits with Month-to-month customers — the
  single largest pool of forward-looking revenue exposure identified so far.
- No CAC / acquisition-spend or gross-margin field exists in this dataset:
  LTV:CAC and payback period are out of scope for this project unless that
  data is sourced separately (flagged as an open data gap).
- No prior model or analysis exists for this project — this is a new build,
  not a rework.

## CONSTRAINTS
### constraints.md
- Use only data/Telco-Customer-Churn.csv and its processed train/test split —
  no external enrichment data is available for this lab.
- Respect the train/test split already made (data/processed/{train,test}.csv,
  seed=42, stratified on Churn) — do not re-split or leak test rows into training.
- TotalCharges must be coerced to numeric (11 blank-string rows -> null) before
  any numeric use; do not silently drop those rows without noting it.
- Do not treat this as a monthly SaaS ledger — it is a single cross-sectional
  snapshot. Any "monthly" rate (e.g. churn hazard) is an approximation and must
  be labeled as such, not presented as a directly observed monthly figure.
- Report churn probability, not a hard yes/no label, so the retention team can
  rank and threshold it themselves (aligns with "ranked risk list" requirement).

## OUTPUT FORMAT
### format.md
- Format: Markdown report with an embedded results table (or CSV export)
  followed by a short narrative summary of top risk drivers.
- Length: concise — under 500 words of narrative; the ranked list itself can be
  as long as the customer base requires.
- Terminology: use "customer" (not "user"), "churn" (not "attrition" or "cancel"),
  "$" for currency, "MRR"/"ARPU"/"LTV" as defined in business.md — do not invent
  alternate metric definitions.
