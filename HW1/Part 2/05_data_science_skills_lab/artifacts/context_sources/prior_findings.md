From Phase 1 (Business Understanding, this lab):
- Contract term is the strongest observed churn-risk dimension: Month-to-month
  42.71% logo churn vs. One year 11.27% vs. Two year 2.83%
  (artifacts/business_metrics.json:churn.logo_churn_rate_by_contract).
- Revenue churn (30.50%) exceeds logo churn (26.54%) — churn is concentrated
  somewhat more in higher-MonthlyCharges accounts, raising the dollar stakes of
  the retention program beyond what a simple customer-count churn rate implies.
- 43.05% of currently-active MRR sits with Month-to-month customers — the
  single largest pool of forward-looking revenue exposure identified so far.
- No CAC / acquisition-spend or gross-margin field exists in this dataset:
  LTV:CAC and payback period are out of scope for this project unless that
  data is sourced separately (flagged as an open data gap).
- No prior model or analysis exists for this project — this is a new build,
  not a rework.
